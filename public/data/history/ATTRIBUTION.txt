Modifications by this project (reproducible with scripts/fetch-history.mjs):
Ramer–Douglas–Peucker simplification of every ring (finest tolerance that keeps a snapshot under 300 KiB), coordinates quantised to 0.01° (≈1 km at the equator), rings crossing the antimeridian split at the seam, rings that collapse dropped, features sorted by area (largest first), closed rings with an explicit repeated first vertex. No borders were invented or edited; anchors are the area centroid of each feature's largest ring. A few edges still span the antimeridian (source pole/unattributed-space rings; `wideEdges` per year): they are kept because in Web Mercator such a chord lies on the map's clip line, and the affected features are flagged `bg`.
