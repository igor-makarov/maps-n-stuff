            An atlas for the curious.

            Maps, straight from the source

              Browse … digitised maps and atlases. Each record links back to its holding collection, with original titles, dates and rights statements. Search places worldwide using OpenStreetMap; archive coverage depends on the maps currently in the catalogue.

              Archive maps and historical borders are separate ways to explore. Archive dates describe publication, with a separate period depicted when documented. Maps with available alignment can be overlaid; other licensed scans remain browseable.

              Sources and rights

              Historical borders: André Ourednik’s Historical Basemaps, GPL-3.0-or-later. The timeline uses 54 published snapshots, rather than continuous annual coverage. Early cultural regions and approximate borders are labelled as such.

              Basemap and place search: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Historical borders are drawn independently of today’s boundaries.

              Explore your way

              Use the keyboard to pan and zoom, browse maps in the results list, or select a region by name in the history view. Links preserve your filters, selected year and map position.

              Meridian Atlas is an independent, open-source project. Its clear typography, spacing and controls draw on the Canton Zürich design system. The name and globe mark belong to this project.
